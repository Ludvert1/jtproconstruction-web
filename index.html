Straight answers

Common questions

Is the estimate really free?
Yes. Free on-site visit, free written itemized quote, no deposit and no obligation. If you'd rather get numbers from three contractors before deciding, that's exactly what you should do — and our quote is itemized so you can compare it line for line.
Are you licensed?
Texas does not issue a state general contractor license — only electrical, plumbing and HVAC are state-licensed trades, and all of that work on our jobs is performed by Texas state-licensed subcontractors. JTProconstruction LLC is a registered Texas LLC and carries general liability insurance; we provide the certificate on request before work starts. Where a city requires contractor registration or a permit, we handle it.
How do you charge, and when?
50% to schedule and buy materials, balance on completion. Everything is itemized in writing before you pay anything — labor and materials separately, so you can see what each part of the job costs.
What if something's wrong after you finish?
Every job carries a 90-day workmanship warranty. Call the same number you've been calling and we come back out. That's the whole process.
How fast can you start?
Most Houston-area jobs get an on-site visit within the same week, and small repairs can often start within a few days of you approving the quote. Larger remodels depend on materials and permits — we'll tell you honestly at the visit.
Do you handle insurance and storm damage work?
Yes. We've done water damage restoration and storm repair work including demo, framing, drywall, paint and trim. We document with photos throughout and provide itemized scopes that adjusters can work from.
Do you work in Austin?
Yes — Austin, Round Rock, Pflugerville, Cedar Park and the surrounding area, run by our Austin crew. Call and ask for the Austin schedule.
